## Title

**BIGTREETECH TMC2209-V1.2 Stepper Motor Drive Module**.[file:134]

## 1. Product Introduction

The TMC2209 is an ultra-silent motor driver IC for two-phase stepper motors.[file:134]

- Continuous drive current: 2 A.[file:134]
- Peak current: 2.8 A.[file:134]
- Compared with TMC2208, the drive current is improved by 0.6 A to 0.8 A and the IC adds locked-motor detection.[file:134]
- Voltage range: 4.75 V to 28 V DC.[file:134]

### Named technologies

- SpreadCycle: highly dynamic motor-control chopper.[file:134]
- StealthChop2: ultra-quiet operation.[file:134]
- MicroPlyer: microstep control.[file:134]
- StallGuard4: locked-motor test.[file:134]
- CoolStep: dynamic current control.[file:134]

### Functional notes

- The interpolation unit can provide 256 microsteps.[file:134]
- StallGuard4 can provide an abnormal signal when the motor rotates abnormally.[file:134]
- CoolStep can save up to 75% energy.[file:134]
- The module uses a standard STEP/DIR interface and is intended to be easy to use.[file:134]

1. The motor is difficult to lose steps.[file:134]
2. Ultra-quiet mode.[file:134]

4. The module can prevent the motor from shaking.[file:134]

### Vref and default settings

- Vref range: 0.2 V to 2.2 V.[file:134]
- Factory default Vref: 1.2 V ± 0.1 V.[file:134]
- Factory default current: 0.9 A.[file:134]

### Potentiometer adjustment notes

- Rotating the potentiometer clockwise reduces the Vref voltage and reduces drive current.[file:134]
- Rotating the potentiometer counterclockwise increases the Vref voltage and increases drive current.[file:134]

## 9. Matters Needing Attention

1. Before installing the driver, disconnect the power supply to prevent burning out the driver.[file:134]
2. Before installing the driver, confirm the installation direction to avoid reverse connection damage.[file:134]
3. Pay attention to the wiring sequence and I/O port; incorrect wiring can prevent the driver from working.[file:134]
4. Do not plug or unplug the driver module while the device is operating.[file:134]
5. When installing the heatsink, ensure it does not contact the pins and cause a short circuit.[file:134]
6. The product is sensitive to static electricity and should be handled carefully.[file:134]
7. Apply heat-dissipation measures such as a cooling pad or cooling fan before use.[file:134]
